## Quality

- A: primary source — official docs, original paper, law, dataset, original report, transcript, original book/chapter.
- B: strong secondary source — textbook, academic review, expert article with citations, reputable publication.
- C: discovery/noise — SEO pages, generic blogs, unsourced summaries, LLM output, weak aggregators.

## Read status

- full: full available text saved.
- relevant_sections: relevant sections saved, not full document.
- partial: extraction incomplete or source partially unavailable.
- abstract_only: only abstract/summary available.
- snippet_only: only search snippet; not evidence.
- inaccessible: could not open.
